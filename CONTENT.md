##Projects
###michiganfgc.com
Using a template from friends with the Ohio FGC, I created a simple website to connect people to their local fighting game community meetups across the state of Michigan!
With the cooperation of local tournament organizers, this website hosts a up-to-date calendar of weekly, monthly, and yearly events with Google Calendar integration — making it easy for tournament organizers to add their events to the site!
